# Hi, I'm AetherAllan

I am a developer working across software products, AI applications, blockchain systems, and experimental games.

My work usually sits between product engineering and systems thinking: turning rough ideas into usable tools, clear interfaces, and technical designs that can be explained, tested, and maintained.

Recently, I have been working on:

- full-stack products from early idea to usable release;
- AI-assisted tools and interactive product experiences;
- blockchain applications, smart contract security, and CTF writeups;
- data visualization and technical systems that make complex behavior easier to inspect;
- game prototypes that combine narrative, interaction, and AI;
- technical notes on engineering, music, tools, and ideas I want to understand more clearly.

This site is my public notebook. Some pages are polished essays, some are technical writeups, and some are snapshots of ideas while they are still forming.
